# R2-D3-02 — Implement internal `checked` evidence policy (ICP-09)
Owner: D3 | Priority: P0 | Gate: R0/R1
Dependencies: R2-D1-00
Allowed paths: services/data-evidence/

## Work
- Keep HTTP-submitted observations unverified; introduce internal checker that checks HTTPS first-party merchant, exact model, price/EUR cents, timestamp/cache, stock/condition and deterministic cross-check against seller source representation; never accept caller asserted checked.
- Store immutable audit trail for decisions and conflicts; support disputed/unverified; keep missing shipping/tax unknown for GE separately.
- Validate first-party, freshness, contradictory, stale, forged checked and replay scenarios; D4 agrees only on semantics, not weaker GE logic.

## Acceptance criteria
- [ ] Fresh first-party independently cross-checked recorded observation may become checked through internal-only decision, with verifiable provenance; ordinary single assertion remains unverified.
- [ ] Tampered/caller checked/replayed/stale/conflicting observations never promote; conflict disputed and timestamps/audit trail intact.
- [ ] GE still blocks missing FI shipping, stock or tax; no unapproved v1 schema edit.

- Integration handoff and known limitations

`checked` means validated evidence at time of scrape, not guaranteed checkout or independent merchant source.
